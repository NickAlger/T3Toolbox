# Lane R8 — uniform tensor layer (`UniformTuckerTensorTrain` + `backend/ut3_*`)

Scope: `t3toolbox/uniform_tucker_tensor_train.py`, `backend/{ut3_constructors, ut3_conversions, ut3_operations,
ut3_linalg, ut3_orthogonalization, ut3_masking, ut3_sampling}.py` (+ `ut3_svd.ut3svd_supercores`, which the brief
named), against `docs/uniform_equivalence_contract.md`, `uniform_masks_vs_ranks.md`, `uniform_supercore_layout.md`,
`uniform_ranks_and_varieties.md`, and the contributor notes (`uniform_internals`, `uniform_polymorphism`,
`uniform_pytree_composition`, `uniform_rank_masks_rationale`, `uniform_svd_prefix_orthogonalization`, `testing_strategy`).

All scripts: `scratchpad/repros/R8/`. Environment: `t3toolbox` env Python, numpy 2.x, jax 0.10.

## How I checked

1. Read every module in scope line-by-line: body vs docstring vs signature shape-comments, every delegate call's
   argument order vs the callee (`t3_to_ut3`→`tucker_change_core_shapes`/`tt_change_core_shapes`, `ut3_*`→
   `apply/entries/probing/sampling_derivatives`, `ut3svd`→`compute_raw_sweep_ranks`, `ut3_add`'s `xnp.block`
   layout vs the mask concatenation order, `ut3_sum_stack`'s merge order vs its mask reshape, `ut3_absorb_weights`
   side convention vs the ragged `t3_absorb_weights`, `_ut3_map_real_weights` double-`where`).
2. `three_prong_sweep.py` — for every public op of `UniformTuckerTensorTrain` (to_dense, to_t3, reverse, scale/neg,
   +/-, squash_tails, sum_stack, inner/norm (both `use_orthogonalization`), sum, entries/apply/probe (ragged and
   packed vectors, W stack), the four orthogonalizations + is_left/right_orthogonal, t3svd (uncapped + capped,
   singular values), rank_adjustment_sweep, minimal_ranks/has_minimal_ranks, unstack/stack) the three prongs:
   (1) per-element dense vs the ragged twin, (2) exact output masks / ranks vs an independently-derived expectation
   (mask concatenation for `+`, rank sums, prefix-ness after SVD, ragged ranks for the orthogonalizations and t3svd),
   (3) garbage-padded input (finite ±10 noise in every padded slot of both supercores) == clean. Matrix: 4
   asymmetric structures with d ∈ {1,2,3,4} × stack ∈ {(), (1,), (2,), (2,3)} × varying-rank stacks (a tree of
   ragged T3s with per-element ranks, packed via `UniformTuckerTensorTrain.stack`) × forced over-padding.
   **Result: everything passes except (a) the garbage prong for `squash_tails`/`+`/`-`/`sum_stack` (every config)
   and (b) every d=1 config (crash).**
3. `sampling_derivs_transposes.py` — `probe/apply/entries_derivatives`, the three corewise transposes and the three
   corewise derivative transposes vs ragged at W ∈ {(), (3,), (2,2)} × C ∈ {(), (2,)} × pad, both `sum_over_probes`,
   ragged and packed vectors, garbage-padded input, plus the docstring's "clean-padded gradient" claim. **All values
   and shapes match ragged and are garbage-robust; the clean-padded claim is false at the boundary bonds.**
4. `weights_ops.py` — `UT3Weights` layer: `from_t3weights`/`to_t3weights`/`from_ut3svd`, `ut3_absorb_weights`
   (garbage transparency on x, on W, on both), `ut3_weighted_norm/inner` (both flags) vs ragged, `reciprocal`/`sqrt`
   (finite, padding exactly 0, masks unchanged, garbage in → same real parts, through gappy masks), `concatenate`/
   `kronecker` exact masks + ragged twins through the gappy masks. **All pass**; one pairing asymmetry found
   (R8-6).
5. `misc_checks.py` — save/load round trip (numpy and jax supercores, gappy masks) keeps host-numpy bool masks;
   masks stay host numpy after every jax op; `ut3svd_supercores` called directly (dense, svals vs ragged,
   `skip_orthogonalization` on a right-orthogonal input, `squash_tails_first=False`); `+`/`inner`/`stack` with
   different padded `N`; `is_*_orthogonal` at d=1; `UT3Masks` value hash/eq.
6. `d1_ops.py` — every op at d=1 vs ragged (ragged d=1 works for all of them).
7. `squash_garbage.py`, `squash_library_garbage.py`, `adam_then_add.py` — the squash/add bug: synthetic garbage, then
   whether library-produced objects carry garbage in the summed slots (SVD-completion cases do not in practice; the
   **corewise optimizer does**).
8. `concat_weights_vs_add.py` — the `W_A.concatenate(W_B)` ↔ `A + B` duality in both layers.
9. Greps for stale markers (`slice`, `3b-`, `dev/archive`, `shape mask`, nonexistent module paths), and the ledgers
   (`deferred_and_rejected.md`, `dev/HANDOFF.md`) for known items.

## Findings

### R8-1 (S, confirmed) — `ut3_squash_tails` sums the *padded* boundary-bond slots into the real slot; `+`, `-`, `squash_tails`, `sum_stack` are not garbage-robust, and the library's own corewise optimizer produces exactly that garbage
`backend/ut3_operations.py:74-88` (`ut3_squash_tails`) calls `tt_operations.tt_squash_tails` on the raw supercore
without masking on entry; `_tt_squash_tails_uniform` (`tt_operations.py:87-114`) does `xnp.sum(G0, axis=-3)` over
**all** `r` left-leg slots of core 0 and all right-leg slots of core d-1. Any nonzero value in a padded boundary-bond
slot is added into real slot 0. Every consumer is affected: `UniformTuckerTensorTrain.squash_tails` (`:241`),
`__add__`/`__sub__` (`:264`, squash after `ut3_add`), `sum_stack` (`:272`). The contract (`uniform_equivalence_contract.md`
"anything in the padding is free"; `testing_strategy.md` prong 3) says garbage padding must not change real parts.

- Synthetic garbage (`squash_garbage.py`): `to_dense` garbage-robust (0.0) but `squash_tails` rel err 30, `x+y` 51,
  `x-y` 60, `sum_stack` 224. `three_prong_sweep.py`: fails in all 42 configs for `+`, 42 for `squash`, 36 for
  `sum_stack` (every other op passes the garbage prong).
- **Supported path (`adam_then_add.py`)**: `x_fit, _ = optimizers.adam(UNIFORM_COREWISE, 'probe', ww, data, x0, rng, batch=20, lr=1e-2, max_iter=50)`
  leaves `max|padded boundary-bond slots| = 0.226` in `x_fit.tt_supercore` (the corewise gradient is nonzero there —
  R8-3), then with `clean = x_fit.apply_masks()` (same tensor): `to_dense`/`norm` agree (0.0) but
  `x_fit.squash_tails()` rel err **0.372**, `x_fit + y` **0.162**, `x_fit - y` **0.158**, `stack(x_fit,x_fit).sum_stack()`
  **0.372**, while `x_fit.to_t3() + y.to_t3()` (ragged) is 7.7e-17. No error, no warning.
- Library SVD outputs (`squash_library_garbage.py`): t3svd/rank_adjustment_sweep/left/right-orth outputs of a
  rank-deficient `x+x` happen to leave those slots at 0 (LAPACK's completion columns) or benign, so the SVD path does
  not trigger it in practice; the optimizer path does.
- Fix: mask the boundary legs on entry in `ut3_squash_tails` (multiply `G0` by `ttm[0]` along axis -3 and `Gf` by
  `ttm[-1]` along axis -1 before summing — or `ut3_apply_masks` the tt supercore); same in `ut3_inner_product`/
  `ut3_norm_orthogonalized` is already fine (they mask first). Add the garbage prong for `+`/`squash`/`sum_stack` to
  `tests/test_uniform_tucker_tensor_train.py` (it has only `test_padding_is_dont_care` for `to_dense`).

### R8-2 (C, confirmed; broader than the ledger entry) — d=1 breaks `squash_tails`, `+`, `-`, `sum_stack`, `norm()`, `inner()`, `t3svd`, `rank_adjustment_sweep`, `left/right_orthogonalize_tt_cores`, `is_left/right_orthogonal`
Root cause: `_tt_squash_tails_uniform` (`tt_operations.py:114`) concatenates `[new_G0, GG_mid, new_Gf]` along axis 0,
so for d=1 it returns **two** cores (`(2,)+stack+(r,n,r)`) from one (ragged `_tt_squash_tails_ragged` handles d=1
correctly). Every uniform op that squashes then crashes deep inside: `__add__`/`squash_tails`/`sum_stack` raise the
`validate()` ValueError ("tt_supercore.shape = (2, 2, 5, 2), expected (1, 2, 5, 2)"), `norm()`/`inner()`/
`left_orthogonalize` raise "all the input arrays must have same number of dimensions" (from `tt_left_orthogonalize`'s
concatenate), `t3svd`/`rank_adjustment_sweep` raise "along dimension 0, the array at index 0 has size 2" (from
`ut3svd_supercores`). Separately `ut3_orthogonality_residual` (`ut3_orthogonalization.py:56-63`) reduces
`xnp.max(..., axis=(0,-2,-1))` over the empty `tt_sc[:-1]`, so `is_left_orthogonal`/`is_right_orthogonal` raise
"zero-size array to reduction operation maximum" even on a d=1 object built without squashing. `to_dense`, `entries`,
`apply`, `probe`, `sum`, `norm(False)`, `inner(False)`, `up_orthogonalize` work (`d1_ops.py`). The ledger
(`deferred_and_rejected.md:41-45`) records only "any uniform *fit* fails in `ut3svd_supercores`" — the cause and
blast radius are larger. Fix: in `_tt_squash_tails_uniform`, when `d == 1` apply both sums to the single core;
in `ut3_orthogonality_residual` skip the TT term when `d == 1` (ragged's `t3_orthogonality_residual` loops over an
empty tuple and is fine).

### R8-3 (D, confirmed) — the corewise transposes are NOT "clean-padded": the gradient is nonzero in the padded boundary-bond slots
`backend/ut3_sampling.py:100-102` and `:200-201` ("return the RAW gradient supercores (dU, dG), clean-padded (the masked
frame zeros the padding, so the gradient never grows rank into it)"). True for every Tucker slot and every interior
bond, false for bond 0's left leg (mode 0) and bond d's right leg (mode d-1): the boundary bond is summed over, so
∂/∂G[l,i,r] for a padded l equals the real-slot gradient. `sampling_derivs_transposes.py`: the "clean-pad tt" check fails
for modes 0 and d-1 in all 6 transposes × both `sum_over_probes` × every config with r>1 (864 failures; no other
failures). Consequence: a corewise update writes nonzero values into those slots (R8-1's realistic path). Fix: either
mask the returned gradient supercores (`ut3_apply_masks`-style on the boundary legs) or correct the docstring and make
R8-1's consumers mask on entry.

### R8-4 (D/C, confirmed) — uniform `+` squashes the boundary bonds, ragged `+` does not: ranks of `x+y` differ between layers, so `W_A.concatenate(W_B)` pairs with `A+B` in ragged but raises in uniform
`uniform_tucker_tensor_train.py:264` (`ut3_squash_tails(ut3_add(...))`) vs ragged `__add__` (no squash):
`concat_weights_vs_add.py`: ragged `(x+y).tt_ranks == (2, 4, 4, 2)` and `t3_absorb_weights(x+y, Wx.concatenate(Wy))`
works; uniform `(ux+uy)` bond-0 mask `[1,0,0,0]` vs `UWx.concatenate(UWy)` bond-0 mask `[1,0,1,0]`, and
`ut3_absorb_weights(ux+uy, UWx.concatenate(UWy))` raises "Inconsistent (UniformTuckerTensorTrain, UT3Weights) pair".
`docs/weighting.md:56` ("`A + B` → … weights `concatenate`") and `ut3_operations.py:368-369` ("the weight-side partner
of `ut3_add`") promise the pairing; it holds only for the backend `ut3_add` (unsquashed). Dense values agree in both
layers (the contract on real parts holds); the divergence is in the rank structure (`to_t3` of the uniform sum has
boundary TT rank 1, the ragged sum has 2). Fix: pick one (drop the squash from the uniform `__add__`, or squash in the
ragged one too, or `ut3_concatenate_weights` squashes its boundary entries to match) and say so in `weighting.md`.

### R8-5 (C, confirmed, low) — `+`/`inner` with different padded `N` fail with raw numpy errors; `ut3_add`'s docstring says the frontend enforces `N`
`backend/ut3_linalg.py:52-53`: "only `N`, `d`, `stack_shape` must match (the frontend enforces that)"; the frontend
(`uniform_tucker_tensor_train.py:258-263`) checks `shape` and `stack_shape` but not the padded `N`. `from_t3(x, N=8) +
from_t3(x)` (a documented way to build objects: "pass to force a larger pad") → `ValueError: all the input array
dimensions except for the concatenation axis must match exactly`; `.inner` → an einsum broadcast error
(`misc_checks.py`). Fix: check `self.N == other.N` with a structural message (or auto-pad the smaller).

### R8-6 (E, confirmed) — `UniformTuckerTensorTrain.stack` of leaves with different padded sizes dies inside `stacking.stack`
`misc_checks.py` / `three_prong_sweep.py`: stacking per-element `from_t3` results with different `n`/`r`/`N` (the natural
way to build a varying-rank stack from ragged T3s — the variety use case) raises numpy's "setting an array element with
a sequence… inhomogeneous shape". A user must know to pass a common `N=, n=, r=` to every `from_t3`. Nothing in
`uniform_ranks_and_varieties.md` or the `stack` docstring (`:775`) says so. Fix: a structural check with a message, or
auto-pad leaves to the common padded sizes in `ut3_stack`.

### R8-7 (D, confirmed) — `up_orthogonalize_tt_supercores` docstring points at a nonexistent module
`backend/ut3_orthogonalization.py:136-137`: "also reused by the orthogonal-representation sweep
(`backend/orthogonal_representations.py`)". No such module (`ls t3toolbox/backend | grep orthogonal_repr` empty); the
reuse is `backend/fv_conversions.py:174`.

### R8-8 (D, confirmed) — `ut3_add` docstring says `shape` is combined "via OR"; it is passed through
`backend/ut3_linalg.py:51` "concatenate the masks (`shape` via OR)". `shape` is the static int tuple and is returned
unchanged (`:58`, `:80`); the in-body comment (`:75-76`) says so correctly. Stale wording from the shape-mask era.

### R8-9 (E, confirmed) — `minimal_ranks` returns jax arrays for a jax-backed UT3
`uniform_tucker_tensor_train.py:653-656` passes `use_jax=self.contains_jax` to `compute_minimal_ranks`, so the rank
metadata comes back as `jax.Array`s (`misc_checks.py`: `['ArrayImpl', 'ArrayImpl']`), against the layer rule that ranks/
masks are host numpy (`uniform_masks_vs_ranks.md` "Masks are host numpy"); `has_minimal_ranks` has to `np.asarray` it.
Fix: drop the flag (ranks are `mask.sum()`, host ints).

### R8-10 (E, confirmed) — `ut3svd_supercores` is public with no caller and no test; it is correct
`misc_checks.py`: direct call on masked supercores + cap masks reproduces `x` (1.3e-15), Tucker/TT singular values match
the ragged `t3svd` in the real slots (extra cap-width slots are exactly 0), `skip_orthogonalization=True` on a
down+right-orthogonal input is exact, `squash_tails_first=False` on a squashed input is exact, and an unmasked
(garbage-Tucker) input is also exact here (docstring says "assumed masked", which remains the right contract). Add a
test or demote to private.

### Verified candidates from the map (all in scope)
- `ut3_absorb_weights` garbage-transparency — **confirmed correct** (garbage in x, in W, in both: real parts equal ragged
  at every config; masks unchanged).
- `ut3_reciprocal/sqrt_weights` guards — **confirmed**: padding finite and exactly 0, real slots = ragged, garbage
  padding neutralized, gappy (concatenated) masks handled.
- sampling ops + corewise transposes + derivatives vs ragged at W and C stacks — **confirmed correct** in value/shape
  and garbage-robust; the docstring "clean-padded" claim is **refuted** (R8-3).
- from_t3/to_t3 round trips with gappy masks — **confirmed** (`to_t3` gathers through `[1,0,1,0]`-style masks after
  `+` for every config; per-element ranks restored).
- to_uniform of a varying-rank stack — **confirmed** via tree→`stack` with common pad; **refuted** as a smooth path
  without a common pad (R8-6).
- `+`/`*` mask algebra then `ut3svd` canonicalization — **confirmed** (exact concatenated masks; t3svd output prefix,
  ranks equal ragged's t3svd ranks per element).
- save/load keeps masks host numpy — **confirmed** (numpy and jax supercores, gappy masks preserved).
- `xnp` on masks / `np` on supercores — none found; masks are host numpy after every jax op tested.
- `ut3svd_supercores` — **confirmed correct**, untested (R8-10).
- stale docstrings / dev-archive error messages — the known ones plus R8-7, R8-8, and the extra slice markers.
